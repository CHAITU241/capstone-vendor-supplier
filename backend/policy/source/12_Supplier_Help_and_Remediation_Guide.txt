 Supplier Help and Remediation Guide
 Synthetic buyer policy corpus | Version 1.1 | 19 September 2026 | Explanatory policy Q&A


 This guide explains the frozen synthetic policies in supplier language. It is an explanatory source, not a
 new requirement catalogue. For exact applicability, fields and thresholds, use the Supplier Category
 Guide, the eight category policies and Evidence and Validation Standards version 1.1.


 HELP-00 Using the guide
 When the assistant explains an individual missing item or rejection, the portal must supply an actual
 structured validation or decision event. The answer then cites the relevant requirement ID and numbered
 check. The examples below are generic and do not assert the state of any real supplier.

 HELP-01 How do I choose a category?
 Use the Supplier Category Guide to find the single primary deliverable in your contract. The assistant can
 suggest a code and cite the definition, but your selection or the onboarding team’s confirmation
 determines the code. For mixed work with no clear primary deliverable, contact the onboarding team for
 classification.

 HELP-02 What documents do all suppliers submit?
 BASE-001 business registration, BASE-002 tax identity/status evidence and BASE-003 bank account
 evidence apply to all 24 subcategories. Your primary code adds exactly the IDs in its category policy; see
 Evidence and Validation Standards for each document’s fields and tests.

 HELP-03 Do I need the same documents as another supplier?
 Possibly not. GOODS-OFF has only the baseline, FOOD-PAN adds FOOD-001, FOOD-CAT adds FOOD-001
 and FOOD-002, and TECH-CYB adds CONF-001, SEC-001, PRIV-001, CONT-001 and INS-CYB-001. The
 confirmed primary subcategory controls the checklist.

 HELP-04 Why do you need cyber insurance?
 For TECH-CYB, SENS-PAY and SENS-DAT, INS-CYB-001 supplies cover for a cyber incident involving the
 contracted service. The certificate must name the registered policyholder, cover cyber liability for at
 least ₹5 crore, be effective, and retain at least 30 calendar days on the evaluation date. Other codes do
 not inherit this requirement unless explicitly mapped.

 HELP-05 What belongs in an insurance certificate?
 See Evidence and Validation Standards §INS-CYB-001 or §INS-PI-001. Both require the policyholder legal
 name, insurer, policy number, specified coverage amount, effective date and expiry date. Cyber cover is
 at least ₹5 crore; professional indemnity cover is at least ₹1 crore. Each needs at least 30 calendar days
 remaining.

 HELP-06 Can I submit missing documents after approval?
 No post-approval deferrals exist in V1. Missing mandatory evidence produces ACTION_REQUIRED. Submit
 the document before the packet can reach READY_FOR_APPROVAL.

 HELP-07 My document was received. Why is it still incomplete?
 A required field may be missing. The portal should provide <ID>.FIELD_MISSING.<FIELD> and identify
 the document and field. Upload corrected evidence; the assistant may explain why the field is required
 using the matching policy section.

 HELP-08 Why was my application rejected?
 A specific failed objective check in the submitted packet produces REJECTED at the final submission gate.
 The explanation must use your actual decision event, observed value, expected value and <ID>.R1 or
 <ID>.R2. Without that event, the assistant cannot know your individual reason.

 HELP-09 Can I fix a rejection?
 Yes. A rejected submission is not a permanent supplier ban. Correct the failed evidence and submit a
 new packet version, which is evaluated on its own date. Example: cyber cover of ₹2 crore fails
 INS-CYB-001.R1 against the ₹5 crore minimum; a corrected certificate can be submitted.

 HELP-10 Why is my case under manual review?
 Unclear, contradictory or unreadable evidence, including a possible legal-name alias, produces
 <ID>.REVIEW when no objective failure has already determined REJECTED. The onboarding team
 examines it. The assistant should identify the uncertainty without pretending it has verified the
 document.

 HELP-11 Can the assistant waive a rule?
 No. There are no automatic waivers or post-approval deferrals in V1. A manual review request does not
 automatically turn failed evidence into a pass. A future exception would need a new policy version.

 HELP-12 How long does onboarding take?
 V1 contains no fixed processing-time promise. A complete, readable packet can reach
 READY_FOR_APPROVAL after the specified checks; ambiguous evidence may require onboarding-team
 review. The assistant should not invent a duration.

 HELP-13 Can I skip the privacy questionnaire if I process no personal data?
 No, where PRIV-001 is mapped to your primary code, the questionnaire is still required. State that no
 personal data is processed, answer its fields, and use the documented None or Not applicable responses
 only where Evidence and Validation Standards §PRIV-001 permits them.

 HELP-14 Is READY_FOR_APPROVAL an approval?
 No. It means every applicable requirement in the submission passed the V1 checks. Supplier approval
 occurs in a separate authorized process; the RAG assistant cannot grant it.


 HELP-15 Example responses
 Missing field example. “We received your cyber liability certificate, but the expiry date is absent.
 INS-CYB-001 requires an expiry date to check that at least 30 calendar days remain. Please upload a
 certificate showing that date.”

 Failed amount example. “Your submission version failed INS-CYB-001.R1: the certificate shows ₹2
 crore of cyber cover and this policy requires at least ₹5 crore. Submit an updated certificate with the
 required cover for a new evaluation.”
