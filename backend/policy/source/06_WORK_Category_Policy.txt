 Workforce and People Services Supplier Policy
 Synthetic buyer policy corpus | Version 1.1 | 19 September 2026 | WORK category applicability


 This category policy covers workforce and people services suppliers whose primary contracted
 deliverable falls in one of the three codes below. Services that source workers, supply people to perform
 work, or deliver learning. Each inherits BASE-001 through BASE-003. The subcategory-specific IDs and
 acceptance checks are set out here and under those same IDs in Evidence and Validation Standards.


 WORK-01 Supplier journeys

   Primary code           Subcategory                            Additional IDs


   WORK-REC               Recruitment                            CONF-001, PRIV-001


   WORK-STA               Temporary and Contract Staffing        CONF-001, PEOP-001, PEOP-002


   WORK-LND               Training and Learning                  TRAIN-001



 WORK-02 Common baseline
 Every supplier in this category must submit BASE-001 business registration evidence, BASE-002 tax
 registration or accepted tax status evidence, and BASE-003 bank account verification evidence. The
 exact fields, alternatives and checks are in Evidence and Validation Standards sections BASE-001 to
 BASE-003.


 WORK-REC Recruitment
 Finding and presenting candidates for roles filled directly by the company. Examples: Recruitment
 agencies, candidate sourcing, and executive search.

 Category boundary. Supplying workers employed or engaged by the supplier to perform company
 work belongs in WORK-STA.

 In addition to the three baseline documents, submit the following evidence. The evidence standard
 named by each ID supplies its fields and exact pass criteria.

 CONF-001 Confidentiality undertaking
 Why and what to submit. Set confidentiality duties for work involving company information. Buyer standard
 confidentiality undertaking signed by both parties.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; buyer name; signatory names; both signatures; execution date.

 CONF-001.R1 The supplier name matches BASE-001 and both parties have signed.

 CONF-001.R2 The execution date is on or before the evaluation date and within the previous 365 calendar days.

 PRIV-001 Personal data handling assessment
 Why and what to submit. Make personal-data use and deletion commitments explicit. Buyer privacy questionnaire,
 signed within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; whether personal data is processed; purpose; data categories; processing
 locations; subprocessors; retention after service end; deletion interval; signature and date.

 PRIV-001.R1 Every field is answered; “None” is allowed for categories and subprocessors, and “Not applicable” is
 allowed for locations, retention and deletion only when the supplier states it processes no personal data.

 PRIV-001.R2 If personal data is processed, retention after service end is at most 24 months and deletion on buyer
 request is within 30 calendar days.


 WORK-STA Temporary and Contract Staffing
 Supplying personnel who perform work for the company under a staffing or contingent workforce
 arrangement. Examples: Temporary workers, outsourced personnel, and contract resources.

 Category boundary. Candidate search alone belongs in WORK-REC. A specialist consulting firm
 delivering a defined advisory output belongs in PROF-CONS.

 In addition to the three baseline documents, submit the following evidence. The evidence standard
 named by each ID supplies its fields and exact pass criteria.

 CONF-001 Confidentiality undertaking
 Why and what to submit. Set confidentiality duties for work involving company information. Buyer standard
 confidentiality undertaking signed by both parties.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; buyer name; signatory names; both signatures; execution date.

 CONF-001.R1 The supplier name matches BASE-001 and both parties have signed.

 CONF-001.R2 The execution date is on or before the evaluation date and within the previous 365 calendar days.

 PEOP-001 Workforce compliance
 Why and what to submit. Confirm that supplier-provided workers have accountable employment controls. Signed
 workforce compliance declaration dated within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; worker engagement model; worker records maintained; wage and contract
 compliance attestation; workforce contact; signature and date.

 PEOP-001.R1 Both the worker-record and wage/contract compliance attestations are Yes.

 PEOP-001.R2 A workforce contact is supplied and the declaration is signed within 180 calendar days.

 PEOP-002 Worker screening
 Why and what to submit. Ensure assigned workers are screened before receiving access. Signed worker screening
 process declaration dated within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; identity check; consent procedure; background check; coverage of
 assigned workers; access timing; signature and date.

 PEOP-002.R1 Identity, consent and background checks are each attested Yes for 100% of assigned workers.

 PEOP-002.R2 The declaration commits to complete the checks before a worker receives access.

 WORK-LND Training and Learning
 Delivering training, workshops, qualifications, or learning services. Examples: Corporate trainers,
 certification providers, workshops, and learning services.

 Category boundary. A standalone learning software subscription belongs in TECH-SW when software is
 the contracted deliverable.

 In addition to the three baseline documents, submit the following evidence. The evidence standard
 named by each ID supplies its fields and exact pass criteria.

 TRAIN-001 Training provider qualifications
 Why and what to submit. Establish that the trainers can deliver the contracted instruction. Provider qualification
 pack with trainer roster, course outline, and trainer credential or experience record.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; trainer names; course topics; credential number/issuer/expiry or dated
 experience history; provider contact.

 TRAIN-001.R1 Each named lead trainer has a current topic-relevant credential OR a dated record of at least 24
 months of relevant delivery experience.

 TRAIN-001.R2 The course outline covers the contracted training topics and a provider contact is named.
