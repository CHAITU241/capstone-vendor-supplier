 Purpose and authority. This is the PDF edition of Supplier Taxonomy V1. A supplier selects one primary
 subcategory for the main contracted deliverable. The assistant may suggest a code using this guide; the
 supplier or onboarding team confirms it. The requirements for the confirmed code come from the
 category policies and the Evidence and Validation Standards.


 TAX-01 Classification rules
 Primary service. Choose the single subcategory that best describes what the contract supplies. A seller
 of laptops is GOODS-ITE; ongoing management of the devices is TECH-ITM.

 Mixed or unclear work. If materially different deliverables make the primary code unclear, send the
 selection to the onboarding team. Do not automatically assign several codes.

 Change in contracted work. Revisit the category when the contracted scope changes; the earlier
 requirement checklist may no longer apply.

 TECH Technology and Digital Services
 Products or services delivered through software, cybersecurity capabilities, or management of company
 technology.

 TECH-SW SaaS and Cloud Software
 Definition. Hosted business applications supplied for the company to use.

 Examples. HR, CRM, expense, collaboration, or project management software.

 Boundary. Security tools whose primary function is to protect or test systems belong in TECH-CYB. Infrastructure
 operated on the company’s behalf belongs in TECH-ITM.

 TECH-CYB Cybersecurity
 Definition. Products or services primarily used to protect, monitor, assess, or control information systems.

 Examples. Antivirus and endpoint protection, SOC services, penetration testing, vulnerability management, and
 identity or access management.

 Boundary. General cloud business software belongs in TECH-SW. Selling network hardware without an operating
 service belongs in GOODS-ITE.

 TECH-ITM Managed IT and Infrastructure Services
 Definition. Ongoing management, support, or operation of company systems, devices, networks, or infrastructure.

 Examples. Managed networks, cloud infrastructure support, IT help desks, and managed workplace services.

 Boundary. A one-time sale of laptops or networking equipment belongs in GOODS-ITE. A standalone security
 monitoring service belongs in TECH-CYB.


 PROF Professional and Advisory Services
 Specialist advice or project work delivered mainly through professional expertise.

 PROF-CONS Business and Technical Consulting
 Definition. Advisory or implementation work on strategy, operations, technology, or engineering.

 Examples. Management consulting, engineering advisory, strategy, and technical implementation consulting.

 Boundary. Ongoing operation of company IT belongs in TECH-ITM. Legal, tax, accounting, or audit work belongs in
 PROF-LFA.

 PROF-LFA Legal Finance and Audit
 Definition. Regulated or specialist advice concerning law, tax, accounting, audit, or corporate finance.

 Examples. Law firms, tax advisers, auditors, accounting firms, and financial advisers.

 Boundary. A supplier processing company payments as a service belongs in SENS-PAY; an insurance broker belongs
 in SENS-INS.

 PROF-MKT Marketing Creative and Research
 Definition. Creative, communications, advertising, or market insight services.

 Examples. Advertising agencies, graphic design, branding, content production, and market research.

 Boundary. A provider whose primary deliverable is an event or venue belongs in FOOD-EVT. A general-purpose
 marketing software subscription belongs in TECH-SW.


 WORK Workforce and People Services
 Services that source workers, supply people to perform work, or deliver learning.

 WORK-REC Recruitment
 Definition. Finding and presenting candidates for roles filled directly by the company.

 Examples. Recruitment agencies, candidate sourcing, and executive search.

 Boundary. Supplying workers employed or engaged by the supplier to perform company work belongs in WORK-STA.

 WORK-STA Temporary and Contract Staffing
 Definition. Supplying personnel who perform work for the company under a staffing or contingent workforce
 arrangement.

 Examples. Temporary workers, outsourced personnel, and contract resources.

 Boundary. Candidate search alone belongs in WORK-REC. A specialist consulting firm delivering a defined advisory
 output belongs in PROF-CONS.

 WORK-LND Training and Learning
 Definition. Delivering training, workshops, qualifications, or learning services.

 Examples. Corporate trainers, certification providers, workshops, and learning services.

 Boundary. A standalone learning software subscription belongs in TECH-SW when software is the contracted
 deliverable.


 FAC Facilities and On Site Services
 Services performed at company premises for site operations, security, or construction.

 FAC-MNT Cleaning Housekeeping and Maintenance
 Definition. Routine cleaning, upkeep, and maintenance of premises or installed facilities.

 Examples. Office cleaning, housekeeping, HVAC servicing, plumbing, and routine electrical maintenance.

 Boundary. Major construction or fit-out work belongs in FAC-CON. Guarding and site security belong in FAC-SEC.

 FAC-SEC Physical Security
 Definition. Personnel or services whose primary purpose is to protect premises and control physical access.

 Examples. Security guards, reception security, access control personnel, and site patrols.

 Boundary. Cybersecurity monitoring belongs in TECH-CYB. Installation of security equipment as a goods purchase
 may belong in GOODS-ITE or GOODS-IND, according to the main deliverable.

 FAC-CON Construction Fit Out and Repair
 Definition. Project-based construction, renovation, fit-out, or substantial repair at company premises.

 Examples. Office fit-outs, civil work, major electrical installations, and renovation contractors.

 Boundary. Routine maintenance belongs in FAC-MNT. Supply of building materials without construction services
 belongs in GOODS-IND.


 FOOD Food Pantry and Hospitality
 Food, drink, catering, and hospitality products or services for company use.

 FOOD-PAN Pantry Coffee and Packaged Consumables
 Definition. Supply of packaged food, drinks, and pantry items without operating a meal service.

 Examples. Coffee beans, tea, packaged snacks, beverages, and pantry stock.

 Boundary. Preparing or serving meals for the company belongs in FOOD-CAT. General office stationery belongs in
 GOODS-OFF.

 FOOD-CAT Catering and Meal Services
 Definition. Preparation, delivery, or operation of prepared meals or food service.

 Examples. Corporate catering, cafeteria operation, and prepared meal supply.

 Boundary. Sealed coffee or snack supply without meal preparation belongs in FOOD-PAN. Event planning with food
 as one component belongs in FOOD-EVT when the event is the main contract.

 FOOD-EVT Events and Hospitality
 Definition. Planning or providing events, venues, conferences, or hospitality services.

 Examples. Conference organisers, event management, venue services, and corporate hospitality.

 Boundary. A contract solely to prepare and serve meals belongs in FOOD-CAT. Creative campaign work without
 event delivery belongs in PROF-MKT.


 LOG Logistics and Supply Chain
 Movement, storage, or fulfilment of goods and parcels.

 LOG-COU Courier and Last Mile Delivery
 Definition. Local parcel, document, or final-mile delivery services.

 Examples. Document couriers, parcel delivery, and local delivery networks.

 Boundary. Freight haulage as the main service belongs in LOG-FRT. Storage and fulfilment belong in LOG-WH.

 LOG-FRT Freight and Transportation
 Definition. Commercial movement of goods over longer routes or with freight operations.

 Examples. Trucking, freight movement, and interstate transportation.

 Boundary. Local parcel delivery belongs in LOG-COU. Managed inventory storage belongs in LOG-WH.

 LOG-WH Warehousing and Third Party Logistics
 Definition. Storage, fulfilment, or managed inventory handling for company goods.

 Examples. Warehouses, fulfilment providers, and third party logistics operators.

 Boundary. Transport only belongs in LOG-FRT or LOG-COU according to the service. Supplying warehouse
 equipment alone belongs in GOODS-IND.


 GOODS Goods and Equipment
 Purchase of physical products where a service is not the main contracted deliverable.

 GOODS-OFF Office and General Supplies
 Definition. Routine office products and general nontechnical supplies.

 Examples. Stationery, office furniture, printing supplies, and general consumables.

 Boundary. Food and pantry consumables belong in FOOD-PAN. IT hardware belongs in GOODS-ITE.

 GOODS-ITE IT AV and Electronic Equipment
 Definition. Physical computing, audiovisual, networking, or other electronic equipment.

 Examples. Laptops, monitors, conferencing equipment, and network hardware.

 Boundary. Managed operation of the equipment belongs in TECH-ITM when it is the main service. Cybersecurity
 products supplied as a security solution belong in TECH-CYB.

 GOODS-IND Industrial Electrical and Safety Equipment
 Definition. Industrial, electrical, mechanical, or personal safety products supplied as goods.

 Examples. Machinery, electrical components, PPE, and industrial tools.

 Boundary. Installation or construction as the main deliverable belongs in FAC-CON. Routine maintenance services
 belong in FAC-MNT.


 SENS Financial and Sensitive Data Services
 Services involving financial transactions, insurance intermediation, or operational processing of sensitive
 information.

 SENS-PAY Payments Banking and Fintech
 Definition. Processing payments or providing banking, payment, or financial transaction services.

 Examples. Payment gateways, payment processors, banking services, and corporate card providers.

 Boundary. Financial advice without payment processing belongs in PROF-LFA. A generic expense software
 subscription belongs in TECH-SW.

 SENS-INS Insurance and Brokerage
 Definition. Providing insurance coverage or arranging and brokering insurance products.

 Examples. Insurers, insurance brokers, and risk placement intermediaries.

 Boundary. Legal or financial advice without an insurance product belongs in PROF-LFA.

 SENS-DAT Data Processing BPO and Identity Services
 Definition. Operating processes on behalf of the company that handle employee, customer, or identity information.

 Examples. Payroll processing, background verification, identity verification, customer support outsourcing, and
 document processing.

 Boundary. Selling a generic software tool without operating the data process belongs in TECH-SW. Supplying
 contract workers to perform work belongs in WORK-STA.


 TAX-03 Assistant guidance
 Cite the relevant subcategory definition when recommending a code. Explain a material boundary when
 two codes could fit. A suggestion is not an approved classification; request onboarding-team confirmation
 if the primary service cannot be determined.
