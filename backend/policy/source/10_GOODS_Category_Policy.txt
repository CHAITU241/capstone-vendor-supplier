 This category policy covers goods and equipment suppliers whose primary contracted deliverable falls in
 one of the three codes below. Purchase of physical products where a service is not the main contracted
 deliverable. Each inherits BASE-001 through BASE-003. The subcategory-specific IDs and acceptance
 checks are set out here and under those same IDs in Evidence and Validation Standards.

 GOODS-02 Common baseline
 Every supplier in this category must submit BASE-001 business registration evidence, BASE-002 tax
 registration or accepted tax status evidence, and BASE-003 bank account verification evidence. The
 exact fields, alternatives and checks are in Evidence and Validation Standards sections BASE-001 to
 BASE-003.


 GOODS-OFF Office and General Supplies
 Routine office products and general nontechnical supplies. Examples: Stationery, office furniture, printing
 supplies, and general consumables.

 Category boundary. Food and pantry consumables belong in FOOD-PAN. IT hardware belongs in
 GOODS-ITE.

 No additional requirement ID applies. The three baseline documents are the complete V1 checklist for
 this primary subcategory.


 GOODS-ITE IT AV and Electronic Equipment
 Physical computing, audiovisual, networking, or other electronic equipment. Examples: Laptops,
 monitors, conferencing equipment, and network hardware.

 Category boundary. Managed operation of the equipment belongs in TECH-ITM when it is the main
 service. Cybersecurity products supplied as a security solution belong in TECH-CYB.

 In addition to the three baseline documents, submit the following evidence. The evidence standard
 named by each ID supplies its fields and exact pass criteria.

 PROD-001 Equipment conformity and warranty
 Why and what to submit. Record supplied equipment specifications, conformity and support. Manufacturer
 specification/conformity sheet and supplier warranty statement.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; manufacturer; model or SKU; conformity statement; warranty duration;
 warranty start event; support contact.

 PROD-001.R1 The model/SKU in the evidence matches the model/SKU offered; a manufacturer conformity
 statement is present.

 PROD-001.R2 Warranty is at least 12 months starting at delivery and a support contact is named.


 GOODS-IND Industrial Electrical and Safety Equipment
 Industrial, electrical, mechanical, or personal safety products supplied as goods. Examples: Machinery,
 electrical components, PPE, and industrial tools.

 Category boundary. Installation or construction as the main deliverable belongs in FAC-CON. Routine
 maintenance services belong in FAC-MNT.

 In addition to the three baseline documents, submit the following evidence. The evidence standard
 named by each ID supplies its fields and exact pass criteria.
