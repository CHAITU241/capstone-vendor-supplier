 Evidence and Validation Standards
 Synthetic buyer policy corpus | Version 1.1 | 19 September 2026 | Authoritative requirement specifications


 This is the authoritative evidence and validation policy for Supplier Requirements Universe V1 version
 1.1. It defines 22 reusable requirement records. Applicability is defined by the eight category policies.
 Each requirement below is a direct citation target identified by its ID.


 EV-01 Interpretation and result codes
 The fictional buyer is based in India and contracts with India-based incorporated entities. INR amounts
 are internal demo thresholds. Evaluate dates in IST; time spans are calendar days and boundary equality
 passes. Issue, signing, review and declaration dates cannot be in the future. Expiry dates may be future.
 Names are compared with the BASE-001 legal entity after ignoring case, punctuation and repeated
 spaces.

 A required document or field that is absent has code <ID>.DOC_MISSING or
 <ID>.FIELD_MISSING.<FIELD>. A failed objective check has code <ID>.R1 or <ID>.R2, matching the
 numbered statements below. Illegible, contradictory or plausibly aliased evidence has code
 <ID>.REVIEW. Record observed and required values and an evidence reference. See Master Policy M-04
 for packet outcomes.

 Only explicit accepted alternatives are permitted. For a signed buyer form, include a supplier signatory
 and date. An item without a specified expiry field is not rejected merely for lacking an expiry. No external
 live verification is performed in the demo.

 BASE-001 Business registration
 Why required. Identify the contracting legal entity.

 Accepted evidence. Business registration certificate or current company registry extract.

 Required fields. Legal name; registration number; issuing registry; registration date; status.

 BASE-001.R1 The registered legal name matches the portal name under the name rule.

 BASE-001.R2 Registration date is on or before the evaluation date and status is Active.

 BASE-002 Tax registration or accepted tax status
 Why required. Record the supplier tax identity and declared registration status.

 Accepted evidence. PAN/tax registration notice; if GST status is not registered, add a signed tax status declaration.

 Required fields. Legal name; PAN/tax reference; GST status (registered or not registered); GSTIN when registered;
 declaration date and signatory when not registered.

 BASE-002.R1 The tax legal name matches BASE-001 and the PAN/tax reference matches the portal entry.

 BASE-002.R2 A registered supplier supplies a GSTIN; a supplier marked not registered supplies a declaration signed
 within 180 calendar days.

 BASE-003 Bank account verification
 Why required. Confirm the account nominated for payment.

 Accepted evidence. Bank-issued account letter or bank statement dated within 90 calendar days.

 Required fields. Beneficiary legal name; bank name; full account number; IFSC; document date.

 BASE-003.R1 The beneficiary name matches BASE-001 and the account number and IFSC exactly match the portal
 payment fields.

 BASE-003.R2 The document date is no more than 90 calendar days before the evaluation date.

 CONF-001 Confidentiality undertaking
 Why required. Set confidentiality duties for work involving company information.

 Accepted evidence. Buyer standard confidentiality undertaking signed by both parties.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; buyer name; signatory names; both signatures; execution date.

 CONF-001.R1 The supplier name matches BASE-001 and both parties have signed.

 CONF-001.R2 The execution date is on or before the evaluation date and within the previous 365 calendar days.

 SEC-001 Information security assessment
 Why required. Establish minimum controls for technology or sensitive-data services.

 Accepted evidence. Buyer security questionnaire, signed within 180 calendar days.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; service scope; security contact; privileged-account MFA; encryption at rest;
 encryption in transit; incident notice commitment; signature and date.

 SEC-001.R1 All three control answers (MFA and both encryption measures) are Yes.

 SEC-001.R2 The committed incident notice interval is at most 48 hours and the signed date is within 180 calendar
 days.

 PRIV-001 Personal data handling assessment
 Why required. Make personal-data use and deletion commitments explicit.

 Accepted evidence. Buyer privacy questionnaire, signed within 180 calendar days.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; whether personal data is processed; purpose; data categories; processing
 locations; subprocessors; retention after service end; deletion interval; signature and date.

 PRIV-001.R1 Every field is answered; “None” is allowed for categories and subprocessors, and “Not applicable” is
 allowed for locations, retention and deletion only when the supplier states it processes no personal data.

 PRIV-001.R2 If personal data is processed, retention after service end is at most 24 months and deletion on buyer
 request is within 30 calendar days.

 CONT-001 Service continuity
 Why required. Demonstrate a practical service recovery plan.

 Accepted evidence. Signed continuity and recovery summary reviewed within 180 calendar days.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; covered service; recovery owner; recovery time objective (RTO); recovery
 point objective (RPO); last exercise date; review date.

 CONT-001.R1 RTO is at most 72 hours and RPO is at most 24 hours.

 CONT-001.R2 The last exercise is within 365 calendar days and the plan review is within 180 calendar days.

 INS-CYB-001 Cyber liability coverage
 Why required. Provide cover for a cyber incident involving the contracted service.

 Accepted evidence. Insurer-issued cyber liability insurance certificate.

 Required fields. Policyholder legal name; insurer; policy number; cyber coverage amount in INR; effective date;
 expiry date.

 INS-CYB-001.R1 The policyholder matches BASE-001, the cover explicitly includes cyber liability and coverage is at
 least ₹5 crore.

 INS-CYB-001.R2 The policy is effective on the evaluation date and expires at least 30 calendar days later.

 INS-PI-001 Professional indemnity coverage
 Why required. Provide cover for errors in professional services.

 Accepted evidence. Insurer-issued professional indemnity insurance certificate.

 Required fields. Policyholder legal name; insurer; policy number; professional indemnity amount in INR; effective
 date; expiry date.

 INS-PI-001.R1 The policyholder matches BASE-001, the cover names professional indemnity and coverage is at
 least ₹1 crore.

 INS-PI-001.R2 The policy is effective on the evaluation date and expires at least 30 calendar days later.

 CRED-001 Professional or service authorization
 Why required. Show authorization relevant to the contracted specialist service.

 Accepted evidence. Relevant provider credential, professional registration or service authorization.

 Required fields. Supplier or named responsible professional; issuer; credential number; authorized service scope;
 issue date; expiry date.

 CRED-001.R1 The authorization scope covers the selected service and the named entity or professional is linked to
 this supplier.

 CRED-001.R2 The issue date is on or before evaluation and the expiry is at least 30 calendar days later.

 PEOP-001 Workforce compliance
 Why required. Confirm that supplier-provided workers have accountable employment controls.

 Accepted evidence. Signed workforce compliance declaration dated within 180 calendar days.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; worker engagement model; worker records maintained; wage and contract
 compliance attestation; workforce contact; signature and date.

 PEOP-001.R1 Both the worker-record and wage/contract compliance attestations are Yes.

 PEOP-001.R2 A workforce contact is supplied and the declaration is signed within 180 calendar days.

 PEOP-002 Worker screening
 Why required. Ensure assigned workers are screened before receiving access.

 Accepted evidence. Signed worker screening process declaration dated within 180 calendar days.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; identity check; consent procedure; background check; coverage of assigned
 workers; access timing; signature and date.

 PEOP-002.R1 Identity, consent and background checks are each attested Yes for 100% of assigned workers.

 PEOP-002.R2 The declaration commits to complete the checks before a worker receives access.

 SITE-001 On site safety
 Why required. Control safety risks in work performed on site.

 Accepted evidence. Signed site safety plan or method statement reviewed within 180 calendar days.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; service scope; hazards; control measures; emergency lead; PPE commitment;
 incident notice interval; review date.

 SITE-001.R1 The plan lists hazards, controls and an emergency lead, and PPE is committed where the plan identifies
 a PPE need.

 SITE-001.R2 Incident notice is within 24 hours and the plan review is within 180 calendar days.

 SITE-002 Physical security authorization
 Why required. Show authority to provide physical guarding and control site access.

 Accepted evidence. Physical guarding authorization and supplier guard-training declaration.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; issuer; authorization number; guarding scope; effective and expiry dates;
 trained-guard attestation; declaration date.

 SITE-002.R1 The authorization scope includes physical guarding and the supplier attests that assigned guards are
 trained.

 SITE-002.R2 Authorization is effective and has at least 30 calendar days remaining; the declaration is within 180
 calendar days.

 FOOD-001 Food supply controls
 Why required. Maintain basic control and traceability of supplied food products.

 Accepted evidence. Signed food sourcing and handling declaration dated within 180 calendar days.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; product types; source and batch traceability; storage method; recall contact;
 recall notice interval; signature and date.

 FOOD-001.R1 Source/batch traceability and documented storage methods are affirmed.

 FOOD-001.R2 A recall contact is named and the commitment to notify the buyer is within 24 hours.

 FOOD-002 Prepared meal controls
 Why required. Add hygiene controls for preparing or serving meals.

 Accepted evidence. Food hygiene certificate for the service site and signed food safety plan.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; site; certificate issuer, number and expiry; allergen controls; temperature
 controls; food-handler training date; plan review date.

 FOOD-002.R1 The certificate identifies the service site and has at least 30 calendar days remaining; both allergen
 and temperature controls are documented.

 FOOD-002.R2 Handler training took place within 365 calendar days and the safety plan was reviewed within 180
 calendar days.

 EVENT-001 Event operations
 Why required. Establish how events are delivered safely.

 Accepted evidence. Signed generic event safety and delivery plan reviewed within 180 calendar days.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; typical event scope; maximum planned attendance; evacuation
 arrangements; first-aid lead; incident notice interval; review date.

 EVENT-001.R1 The plan includes maximum attendance, evacuation arrangements and a first-aid lead.

 EVENT-001.R2 Incident notification is within 24 hours and the review date is within 180 calendar days.

 TRANS-001 Transport and cargo protection
 Why required. Protect goods in the supplier’s custody during transport.

 Accepted evidence. Cargo/transit insurance certificate and signed custody-control declaration.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; insurer; policy number; cargo cover in INR; policy effective and expiry dates;
 tracking method; custody owner; loss notice interval; declaration date.

 TRANS-001.R1 Cover applies to goods in transit and is at least ₹50 lakh; a tracking method and custody owner are
 identified.

 TRANS-001.R2 The policy is effective with at least 30 calendar days remaining; loss notification is within 24 hours
 and the declaration is within 180 calendar days.

 STORE-001 Warehouse controls
 Why required. Account for inventory held at supplier facilities.

 Accepted evidence. Signed warehouse site and inventory-control summary reviewed within 180 calendar days.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; warehouse location; access logging; inventory reconciliation interval; loss
 escalation interval; control owner; review date.

 STORE-001.R1 Access is logged and inventory reconciliation occurs at least every 30 calendar days.

 STORE-001.R2 Loss is escalated within 24 hours, a control owner is named and the summary is reviewed within 180
 calendar days.

 PROD-001 Equipment conformity and warranty
 Why required. Record supplied equipment specifications, conformity and support.

 Accepted evidence. Manufacturer specification/conformity sheet and supplier warranty statement.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; manufacturer; model or SKU; conformity statement; warranty duration;
 warranty start event; support contact.

 PROD-001.R1 The model/SKU in the evidence matches the model/SKU offered; a manufacturer conformity
 statement is present.

 PROD-001.R2 Warranty is at least 12 months starting at delivery and a support contact is named.

 PAY-001 Payment service controls
 Why required. Document controls for payment-service operations.

 Accepted evidence. Signed payment controls declaration dated within 180 calendar days.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; transaction scope; dual authorization; reconciliation frequency; dispute
 contact; dispute escalation interval; signature and date.

 PAY-001.R1 Dual authorization is Yes and transactions are reconciled at least daily.

 PAY-001.R2 A dispute contact is named and escalation is committed within 48 hours.

 TRAIN-001 Training provider qualifications
 Why required. Establish that the trainers can deliver the contracted instruction.

 Accepted evidence. Provider qualification pack with trainer roster, course outline, and trainer credential or
 experience record.

 Required fields. Supplier legal name; trainer names; course topics; credential number/issuer/expiry or dated
 experience history; provider contact.

 TRAIN-001.R1 Each named lead trainer has a current topic-relevant credential OR a dated record of at least 24
 months of relevant delivery experience.

 TRAIN-001.R2 The course outline covers the contracted training topics and a provider contact is named.
