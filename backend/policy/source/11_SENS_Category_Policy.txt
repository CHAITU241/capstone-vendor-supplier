 Financial and Sensitive Data Services Supplier
 Policy
 Synthetic buyer policy corpus | Version 1.1 | 19 September 2026 | SENS category applicability


 This category policy covers financial and sensitive data services suppliers whose primary contracted
 deliverable falls in one of the three codes below. Services involving financial transactions, insurance
 intermediation, or operational processing of sensitive information. Each inherits BASE-001 through
 BASE-003. The subcategory-specific IDs and acceptance checks are set out here and under those same
 IDs in Evidence and Validation Standards.

 SENS-02 Common baseline
 Every supplier in this category must submit BASE-001 business registration evidence, BASE-002 tax
 registration or accepted tax status evidence, and BASE-003 bank account verification evidence. The
 exact fields, alternatives and checks are in Evidence and Validation Standards sections BASE-001 to
 BASE-003.


 SENS-PAY Payments Banking and Fintech
 Processing payments or providing banking, payment, or financial transaction services. Examples:
 Payment gateways, payment processors, banking services, and corporate card providers.

 Category boundary. Financial advice without payment processing belongs in PROF-LFA. A generic
 expense software subscription belongs in TECH-SW.

 In addition to the three baseline documents, submit the following evidence. The evidence standard
 named by each ID supplies its fields and exact pass criteria.

 CONF-001 Confidentiality undertaking
 Why and what to submit. Set confidentiality duties for work involving company information. Buyer standard
 confidentiality undertaking signed by both parties.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; buyer name; signatory names; both signatures; execution date.

 CONF-001.R1 The supplier name matches BASE-001 and both parties have signed.

 CONF-001.R2 The execution date is on or before the evaluation date and within the previous 365 calendar days.

 SEC-001 Information security assessment
 Why and what to submit. Establish minimum controls for technology or sensitive-data services. Buyer security
 questionnaire, signed within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; service scope; security contact; privileged-account MFA; encryption at rest;
 encryption in transit; incident notice commitment; signature and date.

 SEC-001.R1 All three control answers (MFA and both encryption measures) are Yes.

 SEC-001.R2 The committed incident notice interval is at most 48 hours and the signed date is within 180 calendar
 days.

 PRIV-001 Personal data handling assessment
 Why and what to submit. Make personal-data use and deletion commitments explicit. Buyer privacy questionnaire,
 signed within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; whether personal data is processed; purpose; data categories; processing
 locations; subprocessors; retention after service end; deletion interval; signature and date.

 PRIV-001.R1 Every field is answered; “None” is allowed for categories and subprocessors, and “Not applicable” is
 allowed for locations, retention and deletion only when the supplier states it processes no personal data.

 PRIV-001.R2 If personal data is processed, retention after service end is at most 24 months and deletion on buyer
 request is within 30 calendar days.

 CONT-001 Service continuity
 Why and what to submit. Demonstrate a practical service recovery plan. Signed continuity and recovery summary
 reviewed within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; covered service; recovery owner; recovery time objective (RTO); recovery
 point objective (RPO); last exercise date; review date.

 CONT-001.R1 RTO is at most 72 hours and RPO is at most 24 hours.

 CONT-001.R2 The last exercise is within 365 calendar days and the plan review is within 180 calendar days.

 CRED-001 Professional or service authorization
 Why and what to submit. Show authorization relevant to the contracted specialist service. Relevant provider
 credential, professional registration or service authorization.

 Required content. Supplier or named responsible professional; issuer; credential number; authorized service scope;
 issue date; expiry date.

 CRED-001.R1 The authorization scope covers the selected service and the named entity or professional is linked to
 this supplier.

 CRED-001.R2 The issue date is on or before evaluation and the expiry is at least 30 calendar days later.

 INS-CYB-001 Cyber liability coverage
 Why and what to submit. Provide cover for a cyber incident involving the contracted service. Insurer-issued cyber
 liability insurance certificate.

 Required content. Policyholder legal name; insurer; policy number; cyber coverage amount in INR; effective date;
 expiry date.

 INS-CYB-001.R1 The policyholder matches BASE-001, the cover explicitly includes cyber liability and coverage is at
 least ₹5 crore.

 INS-CYB-001.R2 The policy is effective on the evaluation date and expires at least 30 calendar days later.

 PAY-001 Payment service controls
 Why and what to submit. Document controls for payment-service operations. Signed payment controls declaration
 dated within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; transaction scope; dual authorization; reconciliation frequency; dispute
 contact; dispute escalation interval; signature and date.

 PAY-001.R1 Dual authorization is Yes and transactions are reconciled at least daily.

 PAY-001.R2 A dispute contact is named and escalation is committed within 48 hours.
