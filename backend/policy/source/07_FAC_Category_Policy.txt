 Facilities and On Site Services Supplier Policy
 Synthetic buyer policy corpus | Version 1.1 | 19 September 2026 | FAC category applicability


 This category policy covers facilities and on site services suppliers whose primary contracted deliverable
 falls in one of the three codes below. Services performed at company premises for site operations,
 security, or construction. Each inherits BASE-001 through BASE-003. The subcategory-specific IDs and
 acceptance checks are set out here and under those same IDs in Evidence and Validation Standards.

 FAC-02 Common baseline
 Every supplier in this category must submit BASE-001 business registration evidence, BASE-002 tax
 registration or accepted tax status evidence, and BASE-003 bank account verification evidence. The
 exact fields, alternatives and checks are in Evidence and Validation Standards sections BASE-001 to
 BASE-003.


 FAC-MNT Cleaning Housekeeping and Maintenance
 Routine cleaning, upkeep, and maintenance of premises or installed facilities. Examples: Office cleaning,
 housekeeping, HVAC servicing, plumbing, and routine electrical maintenance.

 Category boundary. Major construction or fit-out work belongs in FAC-CON. Guarding and site security
 belong in FAC-SEC.

 In addition to the three baseline documents, submit the following evidence. The evidence standard
 named by each ID supplies its fields and exact pass criteria.

 PEOP-001 Workforce compliance
 Why and what to submit. Confirm that supplier-provided workers have accountable employment controls. Signed
 workforce compliance declaration dated within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; worker engagement model; worker records maintained; wage and contract
 compliance attestation; workforce contact; signature and date.

 PEOP-001.R1 Both the worker-record and wage/contract compliance attestations are Yes.

 PEOP-001.R2 A workforce contact is supplied and the declaration is signed within 180 calendar days.

 SITE-001 On site safety
 Why and what to submit. Control safety risks in work performed on site. Signed site safety plan or method
 statement reviewed within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; service scope; hazards; control measures; emergency lead; PPE
 commitment; incident notice interval; review date.

 SITE-001.R1 The plan lists hazards, controls and an emergency lead, and PPE is committed where the plan identifies
 a PPE need.

 SITE-001.R2 Incident notice is within 24 hours and the plan review is within 180 calendar days.


 FAC-SEC Physical Security
 Personnel or services whose primary purpose is to protect premises and control physical access.
 Examples: Security guards, reception security, access control personnel, and site patrols.

 Category boundary. Cybersecurity monitoring belongs in TECH-CYB. Installation of security equipment
 as a goods purchase may belong in GOODS-ITE or GOODS-IND, according to the main deliverable.

 In addition to the three baseline documents, submit the following evidence. The evidence standard
 named by each ID supplies its fields and exact pass criteria.

 PEOP-001 Workforce compliance
 Why and what to submit. Confirm that supplier-provided workers have accountable employment controls. Signed
 workforce compliance declaration dated within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; worker engagement model; worker records maintained; wage and contract
 compliance attestation; workforce contact; signature and date.

 PEOP-001.R1 Both the worker-record and wage/contract compliance attestations are Yes.

 PEOP-001.R2 A workforce contact is supplied and the declaration is signed within 180 calendar days.

 PEOP-002 Worker screening
 Why and what to submit. Ensure assigned workers are screened before receiving access. Signed worker screening
 process declaration dated within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; identity check; consent procedure; background check; coverage of
 assigned workers; access timing; signature and date.

 PEOP-002.R1 Identity, consent and background checks are each attested Yes for 100% of assigned workers.

 PEOP-002.R2 The declaration commits to complete the checks before a worker receives access.

 SITE-002 Physical security authorization
 Why and what to submit. Show authority to provide physical guarding and control site access. Physical guarding
 authorization and supplier guard-training declaration.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; issuer; authorization number; guarding scope; effective and expiry dates;
 trained-guard attestation; declaration date.

 SITE-002.R1 The authorization scope includes physical guarding and the supplier attests that assigned guards are
 trained.

 SITE-002.R2 Authorization is effective and has at least 30 calendar days remaining; the declaration is within 180
 calendar days.
