 Logistics and Supply Chain Supplier Policy
 Synthetic buyer policy corpus | Version 1.1 | 19 September 2026 | LOG category applicability


 This category policy covers logistics and supply chain suppliers whose primary contracted deliverable
 falls in one of the three codes below. Movement, storage, or fulfilment of goods and parcels. Each inherits
 BASE-001 through BASE-003. The subcategory-specific IDs and acceptance checks are set out here and
 under those same IDs in Evidence and Validation Standards.


 LOG-01 Supplier journeys

   Primary code           Subcategory                               Additional IDs


   LOG-COU                Courier and Last Mile Delivery            TRANS-001


   LOG-FRT                Freight and Transportation                TRANS-001


   LOG-WH                 Warehousing and Third Party Logistics     STORE-001, CONT-001



 LOG-02 Common baseline
 Every supplier in this category must submit BASE-001 business registration evidence, BASE-002 tax
 registration or accepted tax status evidence, and BASE-003 bank account verification evidence. The
 exact fields, alternatives and checks are in Evidence and Validation Standards sections BASE-001 to
 BASE-003.


 LOG-COU Courier and Last Mile Delivery
 Local parcel, document, or final-mile delivery services. Examples: Document couriers, parcel delivery,
 and local delivery networks.

 Category boundary. Freight haulage as the main service belongs in LOG-FRT. Storage and fulfilment
 belong in LOG-WH.

 In addition to the three baseline documents, submit the following evidence. The evidence standard
 named by each ID supplies its fields and exact pass criteria.

 TRANS-001 Transport and cargo protection
 Why and what to submit. Protect goods in the supplier’s custody during transport. Cargo/transit insurance
 certificate and signed custody-control declaration.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; insurer; policy number; cargo cover in INR; policy effective and expiry
 dates; tracking method; custody owner; loss notice interval; declaration date.

 TRANS-001.R1 Cover applies to goods in transit and is at least ₹50 lakh; a tracking method and custody owner are
 identified.

 TRANS-001.R2 The policy is effective with at least 30 calendar days remaining; loss notification is within 24 hours
 and the declaration is within 180 calendar days.

 LOG-WH Warehousing and Third Party Logistics
 Storage, fulfilment, or managed inventory handling for company goods. Examples: Warehouses,
 fulfilment providers, and third party logistics operators.

 Category boundary. Transport only belongs in LOG-FRT or LOG-COU according to the service.
 Supplying warehouse equipment alone belongs in GOODS-IND.

 In addition to the three baseline documents, submit the following evidence. The evidence standard
 named by each ID supplies its fields and exact pass criteria.

 STORE-001 Warehouse controls
 Why and what to submit. Account for inventory held at supplier facilities. Signed warehouse site and
 inventory-control summary reviewed within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; warehouse location; access logging; inventory reconciliation interval; loss
 escalation interval; control owner; review date.

 STORE-001.R1 Access is logged and inventory reconciliation occurs at least every 30 calendar days.

 STORE-001.R2 Loss is escalated within 24 hours, a control owner is named and the summary is reviewed within 180
 calendar days.

 CONT-001 Service continuity
 Why and what to submit. Demonstrate a practical service recovery plan. Signed continuity and recovery summary
 reviewed within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; covered service; recovery owner; recovery time objective (RTO); recovery
 point objective (RPO); last exercise date; review date.

 CONT-001.R1 RTO is at most 72 hours and RPO is at most 24 hours.

 CONT-001.R2 The last exercise is within 365 calendar days and the plan review is within 180 calendar days.
