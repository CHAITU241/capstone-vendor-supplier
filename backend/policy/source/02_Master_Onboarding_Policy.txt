 Master Supplier Onboarding Policy
 Synthetic buyer policy corpus | Version 1.1 | 19 September 2026 | Process and decision policy


 This policy governs the synthetic buyer’s supplier onboarding journey. It defines when the portal asks for
 evidence, how it presents validation findings, and what the knowledge assistant may explain. It applies to
 every India-based incorporated supplier in this capstone. It does not claim to implement an actual
 statutory process.

 All PDFs implement Supplier Requirements Universe V1, frozen version 1.1, and Supplier Taxonomy V1. If
 a copied summary conflicts with the named requirement specification, hold the decision for review and
 correct the source set before further testing. A later revision requires a new version and change log.


 M-02 Classification and applicability
 The supplier selects one primary subcategory using the Category Guide. The onboarding team confirms a
 mixed or unclear selection. Every selected code inherits BASE-001 business registration, BASE-002 tax
 registration or accepted tax status, and BASE-003 bank account verification. Add exactly the requirement
 IDs listed in that code’s category policy. V1 has no spend bands, additional risk attributes or automatic
 stacking of multiple categories.

 Supplier contact information, legal name, tax reference, payment details and chosen code are portal
 fields. The three BASE requirements require supporting evidence. When a supplier changes its contracted
 service, reassess classification and the resulting checklist.


 M-03 Evidence intake
 Accept legible PDF, PNG and JPG evidence in English. Where the specification calls for a signed buyer
 form, require the supplier signatory and date. Use the evaluation date in India Standard Time. All
 intervals use calendar days and values equal to a minimum or maximum threshold pass. Only the
 document alternatives written under the requirement ID are accepted.

 Compare named supplier, policyholder and beneficiary legal entities with the BASE-001 registered legal
 name after ignoring case, punctuation and repeated spaces. A different entity fails; a possible alias or
 subsidiary goes to manual review. The demo does not contact real registries, banks or insurers.
 Contradictory or unreadable evidence goes to review.

 Show all applicable findings. When several states coexist, prioritize REJECTED, then MANUAL_REVIEW,
 then ACTION_REQUIRED. The portal records supplier ID, submission version, primary code, requirement
 ID, check code, observed value, expected value and evidence reference. A correction is a new
 submission version evaluated against its evaluation date.


 M-05 Correction and exceptions
 The supplier can correct missing or failed evidence and submit a new packet. REJECTED identifies the
 failed submission, not a permanent ban. No automatic waivers and no post-approval document deferrals
 exist in V1. A review request cannot itself make a failed mandatory rule pass. The project has no
 published fixed onboarding-time promise; progress depends on complete, clear evidence and review.


 M-06 Knowledge assistant boundary
 The assistant can suggest a category, list requirements for a confirmed code, explain document content,
 answer policy questions and explain actual validation results. It must cite the relevant Category Guide
 code, category-policy code, Evidence Standards requirement ID or master-policy section. It must not
 classify autonomously, extract or verify an upload, execute a rule, issue a decision, approve an exception
 or invent an undocumented requirement.

 For “Why was I rejected?”, use the actual structured decision event and retrieve the matching Evidence
 Standards check. Without a decision event, say that the individual outcome cannot be determined.
 Questions outside supplier onboarding should receive a brief scope response.
