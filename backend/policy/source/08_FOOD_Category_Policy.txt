 Food Pantry and Hospitality Supplier Policy
 Synthetic buyer policy corpus | Version 1.1 | 19 September 2026 | FOOD category applicability


 This category policy covers food pantry and hospitality suppliers whose primary contracted deliverable
 falls in one of the three codes below. Food, drink, catering, and hospitality products or services for
 company use. Each inherits BASE-001 through BASE-003. The subcategory-specific IDs and acceptance
 checks are set out here and under those same IDs in Evidence and Validation Standards.

 FOOD-02 Common baseline
 Every supplier in this category must submit BASE-001 business registration evidence, BASE-002 tax
 registration or accepted tax status evidence, and BASE-003 bank account verification evidence. The
 exact fields, alternatives and checks are in Evidence and Validation Standards sections BASE-001 to
 BASE-003.


 FOOD-PAN Pantry Coffee and Packaged Consumables
 Supply of packaged food, drinks, and pantry items without operating a meal service. Examples: Coffee
 beans, tea, packaged snacks, beverages, and pantry stock.

 Category boundary. Preparing or serving meals for the company belongs in FOOD-CAT. General office
 stationery belongs in GOODS-OFF.

 In addition to the three baseline documents, submit the following evidence. The evidence standard
 named by each ID supplies its fields and exact pass criteria.

 FOOD-001 Food supply controls
 Why and what to submit. Maintain basic control and traceability of supplied food products. Signed food sourcing
 and handling declaration dated within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; product types; source and batch traceability; storage method; recall
 contact; recall notice interval; signature and date.

 FOOD-001.R1 Source/batch traceability and documented storage methods are affirmed.

 FOOD-001.R2 A recall contact is named and the commitment to notify the buyer is within 24 hours.

 FOOD-CAT Catering and Meal Services
 Preparation, delivery, or operation of prepared meals or food service. Examples: Corporate catering,
 cafeteria operation, and prepared meal supply.

 Category boundary. Sealed coffee or snack supply without meal preparation belongs in FOOD-PAN.
 Event planning with food as one component belongs in FOOD-EVT when the event is the main contract.

 In addition to the three baseline documents, submit the following evidence. The evidence standard
 named by each ID supplies its fields and exact pass criteria.

 FOOD-001 Food supply controls
 Why and what to submit. Maintain basic control and traceability of supplied food products. Signed food sourcing
 and handling declaration dated within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; product types; source and batch traceability; storage method; recall
 contact; recall notice interval; signature and date.

 FOOD-001.R1 Source/batch traceability and documented storage methods are affirmed.

 FOOD-001.R2 A recall contact is named and the commitment to notify the buyer is within 24 hours.

 FOOD-002 Prepared meal controls
 Why and what to submit. Add hygiene controls for preparing or serving meals. Food hygiene certificate for the
 service site and signed food safety plan.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; site; certificate issuer, number and expiry; allergen controls; temperature
 controls; food-handler training date; plan review date.

 FOOD-002.R1 The certificate identifies the service site and has at least 30 calendar days remaining; both allergen
 and temperature controls are documented.

 FOOD-002.R2 Handler training took place within 365 calendar days and the safety plan was reviewed within 180
 calendar days.


 FOOD-EVT Events and Hospitality
 Planning or providing events, venues, conferences, or hospitality services. Examples: Conference
 organisers, event management, venue services, and corporate hospitality.

 Category boundary. A contract solely to prepare and serve meals belongs in FOOD-CAT. Creative
 campaign work without event delivery belongs in PROF-MKT.

 In addition to the three baseline documents, submit the following evidence. The evidence standard
 named by each ID supplies its fields and exact pass criteria.

 EVENT-001 Event operations
 Why and what to submit. Establish how events are delivered safely. Signed generic event safety and delivery plan
 reviewed within 180 calendar days.

 Required content. Supplier legal name; typical event scope; maximum planned attendance; evacuation
 arrangements; first-aid lead; incident notice interval; review date.

 EVENT-001.R1 The plan includes maximum attendance, evacuation arrangements and a first-aid lead.

 EVENT-001.R2 Incident notification is within 24 hours and the review date is within 180 calendar days.
