Working-at-height condition
Work above three metres is covered only when a site permit is issued before the work starts. Work without that permit is excluded from the declared working-at-height extension.
The customer issues site permits under its own controls. No issued permit is contained in these originals, so permit compliance for a particular job is not established by this certificate.
